# Butter Editor 0.9.10

- New: split a paragraph into separate paragraphs. Right-click a paragraph's drag handle and choose "Split into paragraphs" to break it at each soft line break. It is the reverse of "Combine into paragraph" on a multi-block selection.
- Drag and drop is smoother and lands more accurately. Long blocks, blocks inside callouts, blocks near the top of a note, and blocks below a `%%comment%%` now reorder cleanly, with the drop placeholder sitting where the block will actually go.
- Drag handles are easier to use. Hovering anywhere across a block reveals its handle, and a block nested inside a callout is easier to grab without the callout's own handle taking over.
- Drag to reorder now works well on mobile.
- Trial and checkout improvements, plus license banner layout fixes on mobile.
